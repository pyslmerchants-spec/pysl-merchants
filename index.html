Working together

What you can expect from us

We want supplier relationships to be clear from the start. Here is how we aim to handle business, orders and communication.

₹

Payment terms discussed upfront

Payment milestones, due dates, currency and any advance are to be written into the quotation acceptance or purchase order before production starts. No one should have to rely on a verbal promise.

▤

LC can be discussed for suitable orders

For orders where it makes commercial sense, we can discuss a letter of credit (LC) or another bank-supported payment arrangement with the buyer and supplier. It depends on buyer acceptance, bank approval, charges and the LC terms.

✓

Written order details

Product specifications, approved sample, quantity, price, delivery schedule, packing, inspection and claims process should be recorded in writing before bulk production.

▣

Proper business paperwork

We aim to work through identifiable businesses and keep the paperwork in order, including quotations, purchase orders, invoices and export or shipping documents where applicable.

↔

One point of contact

Use WhatsApp or email to keep product questions, approvals and production updates in one place. If a problem comes up, raise it early so it can be discussed before dispatch.

Product categories

Products we are interested in

We are currently looking at suppliers for the products below. Exact sizes, materials, quantities and packing requirements will be agreed before an order is placed.

01 · Cotton kitchen & table linen

Home textilesRepeat replenishment
01

Products: tea towels, kitchen towels, table napkins, table runners and coordinated table-linen sets.

  • Declare exact fibre composition; do not label a blend as 100% cotton.
  • Provide finished dimensions, fabric weight/GSM and construction details.
  • Colourfastness, shrinkage and absorbency must be tested or evidenced as agreed.
  • Consistent stitching, hems, colour and dimensions across the lot.
  • Individual units and export cartons must be clean, dry, odour-free and protected.

Helpful to have: consistent production, batch identification, a sample before production and packing details for each product.

02 · Block-printed cushion covers

Printed textilesDesign-led
02

Products: cushion covers, decorative pillow covers and coordinated soft-furnishing textile items.

  • State fabric composition, weave, GSM, finished size and tolerances.
  • Print alignment, repeat, colour and placement must match the approved sample.
  • Wash/rub colourfastness and shrinkage must be tested or agreed before order.
  • Secure seams, clean finishing and functional, snag-free zippers or closures.
  • Disclose hand-print variation; do not pass unapproved shade/design changes.

Helpful to have: control over print colours, a sample for approval and clear washing/care information.

03 · Small metal home décor

Home accessoriesGiftware
03

Products: decorative trays, candleholders, bowls, small planters and compact metal accessories.

  • Declare base metal/alloy, plating or coating and finish; no unsupported material claims.
  • Dimensions, weight, wall thickness where relevant and finish must match the approved sample.
  • No sharp burrs, unstable bases, exposed hazardous edges or loose components.
  • Coating must be uniform; corrosion/tarnish resistance evidence where specified.
  • Use protective wrapping and tested carton packing to prevent scratches and movement.

Helpful to have: batch records, finish checks, suitable protective packaging and details of the metal used.

04 · Cotton tote & fabric bags

Reusable bagsPromotional products
04

Products: reusable cotton totes, printed fabric bags, shopping bags and branded fabric gift bags.

  • State fibre composition, fabric GSM, bag dimensions and handle length/width.
  • Seams, handles and stress points must withstand the agreed load test.
  • Print must be aligned, cured and resistant to the agreed rub/wash conditions.
  • No stains, holes, broken stitching, loose threads or strong chemical odour.
  • Custom branding only from buyer-approved artwork and written permission.

Helpful to have: reinforced handles, consistent printing, a retained approved sample and clear carton quantities.

Minimum supplier requirements

What we check before working with a supplier

Sending an enquiry does not mean automatic approval. We may ask for documents, product samples, a workshop video or a visit before confirming whether we can work together.

Basic requirements

  • Legally identifiable business with verifiable name, address and responsible contact.
  • Ability to issue a formal quotation and tax invoice as applicable.
  • Clear product specifications, MOQ, capacity and production lead time.
  • Willingness to supply samples and accept written inspection criteria.
  • Agreement to disclose subcontracting and obtain approval before changing production location.
  • Ability to segregate rejected goods and carry out corrective action.
  • Acceptance of buyer-approved sample/specification as the bulk production reference.
  • Honest declarations about materials, processes, testing and certifications.

Documents and details we may ask for

  • Business registration / proprietorship or entity details and GSTIN, where applicable.
  • Bank proof matching the supplier's legal/business name.
  • Product catalogue, recent product photos and factory/workshop address.
  • Written price sheet, MOQ, sample charge, capacity and lead times.
  • Relevant test reports, material declarations and valid certificates, if claimed or required.
  • Sample inspection record and defect-handling procedure.
  • Packaging details, carton dimensions, gross/net weight and pack quantity.
  • References or evidence of prior supply, where available.

Not every product needs a certificate. We will tell you if a buyer, product or applicable rule requires one. Please only share genuine, valid documents.

Supplier onboarding SOP

What happens if we move forward

We check the business details and quotation first, then review samples and quality. We may ask for more information or changes before making a decision.

STAGE 01

Business details and documents

We check your business details, products and the documents requested. We may come back to you if anything is missing.

STAGE 02

Product fit and pricing

We review your prices, minimum quantities, capacity, lead times and packing details.

STAGE 03

Sample check

We compare the sample with the agreed product details and note any issues that need fixing.

STAGE 04

Supplier decision

Any approval applies to the product reviewed. It is not a promise of an order or a particular quantity.

STAGE 05

Order confirmation and approved sample

Before production, the order should set out the approved sample, product details, quantity, delivery dates, packing and payment terms.

STAGE 06

Production updates

Keep us informed about progress and tell us early if there is a delay, shortage or quality concern.

STAGE 07

Final quality check

We check the agreed quantity and key quality points. Where required, goods should not be dispatched until the check is cleared.

STAGE 08

Dispatch and follow-up

We check quantities, labels, cartons and shipping documents, then record any issues that need follow-up.

Non-negotiable change control: no material, supplier, factory, process, artwork, colour, dimension or packaging substitution without written approval. Verbal approval alone is not a valid change record.

Supplier review · 100 points

  • Quality consistency & sample match — 30 points
  • Price transparency & quotation completeness — 15 points
  • Delivery reliability & production capacity — 15 points
  • Documentation and traceability — 15 points
  • Communication and change control — 10 points
  • Packaging and dispatch readiness — 10 points
  • Corrective action and improvement — 5 points

Our proposed benchmark: 75/100, with all basic requirements met. Serious safety, honesty or product-specification issues can rule out a supplier regardless of score.

How we handle quality problems

  1. Record defect, lot, photos and affected quantity.
  2. Contain/segregate affected stock; do not dispatch it.
  3. Supplier submits root cause and corrective/preventive action.
  4. PYSL reviews evidence and may request reinspection or replacement.
  5. Close the issue in writing; repeated critical failures may suspend supplier approval.

Any costs for rework, replacement, inspection or delays must be agreed in the supplier agreement or purchase order.

Quotations

What to include in your quotation

Please include the details below so we can assess your offer without repeated follow-up.

Please include

  • Supplier legal name, address, GSTIN if applicable, contact person and date.
  • Product code, description, material/composition and specification version.
  • Unit price, currency, tax treatment and price validity.
  • MOQ per SKU, sample price and sample lead time.
  • Production capacity per month and committed production lead time.
  • Packaging details, units per carton, carton size and gross/net weight.
  • Payment terms and quotation's delivery basis/Incoterm where relevant.
  • Available test reports/certifications, clearly stating scope and validity.

A few important points

  • Communicate delays or quality concerns immediately and in writing.
  • Never substitute raw materials, subcontractors or specifications without approval.
  • Keep buyer designs, prices and business information confidential.
  • Do not use buyer logos, artwork or photos publicly without permission.
  • Maintain accurate production and inspection records for each order.
  • Please be specific about any certification or standard you claim, and share the supporting document.
  • Agree commercial terms, claims process and governing documents before production.
